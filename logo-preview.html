Architectural Design Philosophy

1. Apple Continuous Curvature (G2 Squircle)

Outer substrate follows Apple's superellipse algorithm, removing abrupt corner transitions to create organic continuity with modern high-DPI displays.

2. Emil Kowalski Specular Light Catch

Tactile 1px top-edge hairline catches simulated incident light, anchoring the icon in physical space rather than flat digital pixels.

3. Faceted Security Armor

Dual-tone obsidian armor facets symbolize on-chain protection against rugpulls, sudden mints, and stealth liquidity drains on BNB Chain.

4. Gemini AI Neural Nexus

Precision 4-point neural starburst and constellation graph represent real-time Viem RPC telemetry scanning smart contract bytecode.
