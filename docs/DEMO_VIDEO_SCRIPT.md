## 🎙️ Recording Setup & Best Practices
1. **Screen Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p) or 2560x1440 (2K), 60 FPS.
2. **Audio Mix:** Voiceover at 100%, background music (futuristic lo-fi tech) at 12–15%.
3. **Voice Generator Option (If using AI Voice):** Use [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) with the *Adam* or *Antoni* voice (Stability: 50%, Clarity: 75%).
4. **Browser Setup:** Chrome/Brave in Full Screen (`F11`), bookmarks bar hidden, zoom at 100%. Open `http://localhost:5173` with MetaMask connected to BNB Testnet.
