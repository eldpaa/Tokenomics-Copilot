- Pull liquidity from unlocked automated market maker (AMM) pools.
- Trigger hidden 99% transfer sell taxes on BEP-20 tokens.
- Mint billions of unbacked tokens directly to developer wallets.

Traditional security audits cost upwards of **$15,000** and require weeks of lead time. Retail investors trading on DEXes like PancakeSwap, Thena, or Biswap have no automated protection.
